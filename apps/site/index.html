Quick answers

What can I send?

Anything a person can hand over, up to 25 kg. We skip cash, alcohol, medication and weapons.

Who can send it?

Anyone in Accra with a phone. Mum, your cousin, the shop that has your order. A rider calls them to collect.

Where do I collect?

On campus, off campus or in Berekuso. You choose when you order.

How do I pay?

MoMo or card, in the app. HELLOHILL comes off the delivery fee of your first one.

Run a business on campus?

We bring your stock up from Accra in one trip, or list your shop for students. We don't deliver between halls.
